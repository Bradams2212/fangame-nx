Linux/GitHub Actions build for fangame-nx (rgss-switch engine).

Files in this bundle (copy into the ROOT of your fork of theflash48/fangame-nx):
  .github/workflows/switch-build.yml
  ci/*.sh, ci/apply_mkxpz_patches.py, ci/extra/perfil-gs-vitro.json

Result: artifact "rgss-switch-release" (the engine zip) and "build-logs".

compile/link step. The patch stage and the packaging layout were tested.
